Discover a **relaxed watercolor workshop in an inspiring venue** : the fields of the organic farm of Cultivo Puro. This creative 2-hour experience is designed for anyone who wants to **slow down, experiment, and connect with nature & watercolor** in a welcoming atmosphere in the middle of the fields.

**Practice, experiment, and create your own piece** with personal support throughout the session.

**No previous experience is needed**, making the workshop suitable for complete beginners, travelers, creatives, and anyone curious to try watercolor for the first time.
**All materials are included**, and you will leave with your own **handmade artwork** and a creative memory of Santa Cruz.
