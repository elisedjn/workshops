Discover a **relaxed and deeply meaningful kintsugi workshop** in Santa Cruz, where the ancient Japanese art of repairing ceramics becomes a metaphor for healing and self-discovery. This **3-hour experience** invites you to embrace the philosophy of **reconnecting with your own pieces, celebrating imperfections, and transforming scars into golden beauty**—just as kintsugi honors the history of an object by highlighting its breaks with precious materials.

Please bring a ceramic piece of your own: either something already broken at home, like that **favorite mug you’ve been meaning to fix one day**, or a ceramic object you’re okay to break and rebuild during the workshop. It can be a mug, plate, cup, bowl, soap tray, decorative object, or another small ceramic piece.

During the session, you will learn a mindful, accessible approach to **repairing, honoring, and sublimating broken lines with authentic materials**. The workshop uses **traditional urushi lacquer and golden mika powder**, rare and precious elements that elevate both the process and the final piece. This is not about perfection or traditional mastery, but about **slowing down, embracing imperfection, and giving new life to what was once broken**.

**No previous experience is needed**—this workshop is perfect for beginners, seekers of mindfulness, and anyone drawn to the therapeutic power of kintsugi. **All repair materials are included**, and you will leave with your own **golden-repaired ceramic piece**, a tangible reminder of the beauty in healing.
