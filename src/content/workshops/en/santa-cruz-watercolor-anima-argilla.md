**Discover a relaxed watercolor workshop in a charming ceramic shop**, where creativity and craftsmanship meet. This 3-hour experience is designed for anyone who wants to **reconnect with their artistic side, experiment, and explore the joy of watercolor** in a cozy, inspiring space surrounded by beautiful ceramics.

**Practice, play, and create your own piece** with gentle guidance and personal support throughout the session.

**No previous experience is needed**—this workshop is perfect for beginners, dreamers, and anyone who has always wanted to try watercolor but never dared. **All materials are included**, and you’ll leave with your own **handmade artwork** and a renewed sense of creative confidence.
