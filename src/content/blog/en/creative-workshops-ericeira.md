Ericeira is often associated with surf, ocean views, and slow afternoons in the village. A creative workshop fits naturally into that rhythm because it gives visitors and locals a quiet moment to make something with their hands.

For families, it can become an easy shared activity between beach days. For groups of friends, it offers a different way to spend time together without needing previous artistic experience.

The best workshops are simple to join, generous with materials, and flexible enough for each person to leave with something personal.
